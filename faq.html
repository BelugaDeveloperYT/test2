Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about MyBot.

What is MyBot? +

MyBot is a Discord bot designed to provide useful tools and features for Discord servers.

How do I add MyBot to my server? +

Click the "Add to Discord" button on our homepage and follow the authorization steps provided by Discord.

Is MyBot free? +

MyBot is currently free to use. Some features or services may change in the future.

What permissions does MyBot need? +

MyBot only needs the permissions required for the features that you choose to use. You can review the requested permissions during the Discord authorization process.

Can I remove MyBot from my server? +

Yes. You can remove MyBot from your Discord server at any time using your server's Discord settings.

Does MyBot store my messages? +

MyBot's data practices depend on the features being used. Please read our Privacy Policy for information about what data may be collected and how it is used.

How can I get support? +

If you need help, you can join our support server or contact the MyBot support team.

Where can I read the Terms of Service? +

You can read our Terms of Service by visiting the Terms page using the navigation menu above.
